Heart Health Tips for Tamil Nadu Residents: Expert Advice from Dr. Siva Kumar

Heart disease is increasingly common among Tamil Nadu's population due to changing lifestyles and dietary habits. Dr. Siva Kumar, our leading cardiologist at Medinova Chennai, shares essential tips to maintain a healthy heart and prevent cardiovascular diseases.

1. Follow a Tamil Nadu Friendly Heart-Healthy Diet

While traditional Tamil cuisine is delicious, be mindful of coconut oil, refined rice, and excess salt. Opt for brown rice, millets (kambu, ragi, samai), use minimal coconut oil, include more vegetables, and choose steamed over fried foods. Limit outside food and reduce sugar intake in filter coffee and sweets.

2. Stay Physically Active in Tamil Nadu Climate

With Tamil Nadu's warm climate, exercise early morning or evening. Aim for 30-45 minutes of walking, yoga, or cycling daily. Use public parks in your neighborhood like Marina Beach walkway, Race Course in Coimbatore, or Teppakulam in Madurai for morning walks.

3. Regular Health Checkups are Crucial

Tamil Nadu has a high prevalence of diabetes and hypertension. Get your BP, blood sugar, and cholesterol checked regularly. Medinova offers affordable health checkup packages starting from just ₹999 at all our branches across Tamil Nadu.

4. Quit Smoking and Limit Alcohol

Smoking and tobacco use (including pan masala and gutka) significantly increase heart disease risk. If you smoke, seek help to quit. Limit alcohol consumption as excessive drinking leads to high BP and heart problems.

5. Manage Work Stress

With IT hubs in Chennai and Coimbatore, work-related stress is common. Practice meditation, take regular breaks, spend time with family, and ensure work-life balance. Join yoga classes available across Tamil Nadu cities.

6. Get Quality Sleep

Most adults need 7-8 hours of sleep. Avoid late-night screen time, maintain a regular sleep schedule, and keep your bedroom cool and dark for better sleep quality.

7. Know the Warning Signs

Learn to recognize heart attack symptoms: chest pain/pressure, pain in left arm/shoulder/jaw, shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea. If you experience these, call 108 immediately or rush to the nearest Medinova emergency center.
